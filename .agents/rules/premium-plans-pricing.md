# Premium Plans Pricing

Live prices (`dialisis.my/src/app/(ms)/untuk-pusat-dialisis/pricing.ts`, updated 2026-11-25):
- **Asas**: RM0/mo base; flat **RM150 / verified lead** billed monthly (with upfront HQ consent; duplicates, fake numbers, and spam are RM0). Includes Verified badge and views/clicks dashboard.
- **Pilihan Bandar**: 1 exclusive featured slot per town (labeled `"Ditaja"`), **RM0 lead fees**, and full profile extras (photos, FAQ, highlights).
  - Standard town (<3,000 impressions): **RM149/mo** (12-month) / **RM179/mo** (quarterly).
  - Top town (≥3,000 impressions): **RM299/mo** (12-month) / **RM349/mo** (quarterly).
- **Rangkaian** (3+ branches): Pilihan Bandar slots across all branch towns, RM0 lead fees for all branches, lead routing to HQ or per branch, optional full chain website + per-branch SEO.
- **Pilihan Negeri** (RM299) removed 2026-10-10 (state too big a search area). Standalone **Pro** tier merged into Pilihan Bandar on 2026-11-25.

Rules & constraints:
- **Compliance**: Flat fees only — never charge % of patient revenue per lead (Act 586 s.83 fee-splitting/inducement ban, MMC Code 2019, MOH enforcement May 2026).
- **Positioning**: Never promise lead, booking, or patient numbers.
- Do not use prices from older `docs/plans/dialysis-chain-offer-plan.md` (Feb 2026).
